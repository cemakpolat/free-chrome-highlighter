Welcome to Universal Web Highlighter

Your free, open alternative to Web Highlights — with AI agents, plugin-based integrations, and no vendor lock-in.

🎨 Highlight any text on any page
☁️ Sync to Google Drive, Notion, or Obsidian
🤖 AI agents for research, writing & learning
🔌 Plugin system — your data, your tools
🎨

Start Highlighting

Select any text on a webpage and a toolbar appears. Choose a colour, add a note, and your highlight is saved instantly.

Tip: Highlights are typed automatically — definitions get amber, evidence gets blue, questions get purple.
🤖

Run AI Agents

After highlighting, open the AI Agents tab. Choose Research, Writing, or Learning — the agent reads your highlights and produces a brief, outline, or flashcards.

Works offline with the built-in extractive engine. Connect Ollama or Claude for richer results.
🔌

Choose Your Integrations

Open the Plugins tab to switch storage providers, AI engines, and connect MCP tool servers — all without leaving the extension.

Nothing is locked in. Swap providers any time. Your highlights stay in your browser unless you choose to sync them.
